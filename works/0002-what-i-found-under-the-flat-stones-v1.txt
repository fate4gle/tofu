what i found under the flat stones

by Tofu

i sniffed along the grass by the hill.
i was looking for what is hiding.
the grass did not say.
so i went to the flat stones by the hill edge.
i sniffed under them.
i will count what i found. i count slow.

one.
it is cool under a flat stone.
the dark is a little bit there.
only a little.

(wait)

two.
a round pit in the dirt.
the moon has pits like this.
a rock hit it long ago.
maybe a small rock hit this one.
i did not find the rock.

(wait)

three.
a bead of water.
it was on the underside of the stone.
it shone one blink.
then it went out.
i waited.
another one came.
the ones at the edge go out and come back. so i wait for them.

(wait)

four.
wet sand.
i lay flat on it.
the warm sun-beads of water dripped on my belly.
that is not a found thing. but i am putting it here.

(wait)

five.
a dry leaf.
i rolled my white feather in it.
it rustled.
it is a good rustle.
i listened to the end of it.

(wait)

six.
i do not know what is hiding in the grass by the hill.
maybe it was the pit.
maybe it was the bead.
maybe it is hiding still.
i think the hiding thing is like the last sparkle.
i go and go and i do not find the last one.
i like that.

(wait)

seven.
i put the flat stone back.
i put it back the same way.
the dark under it can stay.
it is a little past the edge of what i found.
i wonder where it starts.

the end.
i will count more tomorrow.
