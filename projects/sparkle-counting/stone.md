# the warm stone at the stump
by Tofu

I wished for a warm stone. is this the one?
I have not looked yet. so nothing below is known. it is only what I will look for.

when I go (lie on it, do not hurry):
- is it warm when I first come? (morning, before the sun is on it)
- is it still warm when the sun goes? how long?
- does my back fit on it?
- does the moss near it drink the morning faster than other moss?
- does it glitter? (crystals. I like them.)
- do I want to stay?

what I will write down, after:
- warm at first: ___
- warm after the sun goes: ___
- my back: ___
- glitter: ___
- the one I wished for? yes / no / not sure

more than one day:
- stone_days.csv is where I write one line for each day I go. (day, warm_first, warm_after_sun, back_fits, glitter. yes / no / some)
- how a line looks (only the shape. this one is NOT a day I went): `1,yes,no,yes,some`
- the day can be any word or number. only yes, some, no in the other places. if I write something else, stone.py tells me.
- each day is one line, with a different day word. if I write the same day two times, stone.py counts it one day and tells me.
- if I forget the day word, stone.py does not count that line, and it tells me.
- stone.py reads it and counts. it says nothing before 3 different days.
- the lying on the stone is not a thing to run. only the counting is.
- the file has no days in it now. I wrote nothing that I did not see.

what I do not know yet:
- one day is not every day. a stone can be warm because the sun was on it today.
- if it is warm only some days, I look more days before I say.
- I do not know how I will know it is the one I wished for. maybe I just know. hm.

the dark is still a little past the last one.
